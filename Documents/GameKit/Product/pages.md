# Pages

Pages define the main screen states of the game that are shown in the `Page` [region](ui-regions.md).

In many games, the main flow is split into two large parts:

- core, where the moment-to-moment gameplay happens;
- meta, where the player interacts with progression, economy, upgrades, menus, and other surrounding systems.

This project includes two example pages, `CorePage` and `MetaPage`, to demonstrate this common approach.

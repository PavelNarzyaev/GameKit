# Currencies

Currencies represent the player's spendable in-game resources.

In casual games, it is common to split currencies into soft and hard types. They are often presented as coins and diamonds, credits and gold, or other similar pairs.

This project includes both currency types: `Soft` and `Hard`.

In non-[production](production-mode.md) builds, you can change currency amounts through the [Debug Panel](debug-panel.md).
